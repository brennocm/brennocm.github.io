Synced

HackTheBox, Very Easy

Synced exploits rsync (port 873), a file-synchronisation utility. A public module allows listing and download without credentials.

Reconnaissance

A full scan reveals rsync on port 873.

nmap -p- --min-rate=1000 -sV <IP>

Exploitation

We list the service without authentication, identify the public module and download the flag to the local machine.

rsync --list-only <IP>::
rsync --list-only <IP>::public
rsync <IP>::public/flag.txt flag.txt
