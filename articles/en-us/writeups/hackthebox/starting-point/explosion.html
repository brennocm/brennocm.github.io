Explosion

HackTheBox, Very Easy

Explosion introduces RDP (Remote Desktop Protocol) on port 3389. The host accepts a connection as Administrator with a blank password.

Reconnaissance

Nmap identifies the RDP service on port 3389.

nmap -sV <IP>

Exploitation

With the xfreerdp client we open the remote session. It is accepted as Administrator with a blank password, due to misconfigured credentials.

xfreerdp /v:<IP> /cert:ignore /u:Administrator

On the desktop, the flag is retrieved.
